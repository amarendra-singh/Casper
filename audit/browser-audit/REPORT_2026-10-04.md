### F01 — P1: customer blocking and recovery actions are built from cohorts

**Evidence:** `C:\WorkStation\Projects\Python\Casper\backend\app\services\fraud.py:733` groups returned events by state, return reason and signal, rather than a stable customer identity. `C:\WorkStation\Projects\Python\Casper\backend\app\services\action_pipeline.py:26` assumes ₹600/order; `:53` assigns BLOCK; `:89` calls cohort frequency a repeat offender. `C:\WorkStation\Projects\Python\Casper\frontend\src\pages\Dashboard.jsx:147` recommends “Block repeat refusers.”

**Reproduce:** Open Dashboard action queue and Fraud actor view. Live data showed 21 actor patterns, two BLOCK cohorts, including 17/17 Misshipment and 3/3 missing-item events, and 13 repeat refusers. Run the synthetic business evidence: five returns without monetary values produce ₹3,000 recovery. **Actual:** cohort hashes become customer actions and invented amounts become recoverable impact. **Expected:** cohort investigation only; customer restrictions require verified customer identity, sufficient evidence, permissions and review. Recovery must derive from documented eligible losses/claims, with estimate assumptions explicit.

**Fix/check:** Separate cohort signals, identified actors and claim cases. Do not export cohort hashes as customer blocklists. Test mixed customers sharing state/reason, ordinary orders, sparse observations and absent order amounts. Evidence: `dashboard-actions.png`, `audit-dashboard-controls.receipt.json`, `current-business-evidence.json`. Confirms earlier A01/A04.

## Design recommendations and unverified risks

These are not additional reproduced defects:

- Define a metric registry: name, source, company/platform/date scope, denominator, cost coverage, rounding and unavailable state. Use weighted aggregates where intended; average SKU margins need an explicit definition.
- Snapdeal's12 unmatched SKUs and ShopDeck's absent SKU results currently produce zero-valued matched-subset profit cards. Coverage warnings exist for Snapdeal, but headline values should distinguish an empty computable subset from proven zero business profit.
- Make company settings distinct from consolidated reporting. Add provenance and validation to overrides, effective-date defaults and cost recalculation.
- Practical inventory needs stock movements/reservations/count adjustments; a catalog view alone does not demonstrate these capabilities. Claims/actions need durable assignee, status, evidence, deadlines and measured recovery outcomes, not just a derived queue.
- Keep imports previewable and transactional, with parser version/source identity, overlap policy and rollback. Full bill/import operator journeys still require disposable browser fixtures.
- Financial models use floating-point fields; cent precision is a risk needing targeted round-trip tests, not a reproduced drift defect here.
- PostgreSQL/deployment behavior, concurrency, shared rate limiting, background import jobs and restart/session recovery were not verified. Process-local controls and database-specific SQL deserve deployment tests before scale claims.
- No legal/tax compliance opinion, penetration test or comprehensive accessibility certification is provided.
